The four boundary crossings

Same components, both directions

01

Sense & Assemble

GNSS fixes the vehicle's position; the baseband processor packages it with heading, speed, and brake state into a Basic Safety Message.

02

Sign & Transmit

The HSM signs the packet with a rotating pseudonym certificate, then the radio broadcasts it over the 5.9GHz sidelink — no cell tower involved.

03

Receive & Verify

The other vehicle's radio picks it up directly; its own HSM checks the signature against a trusted certificate before anything downstream acts on it.

04

Decide & Act

The safety stack checks the trajectory, posts a result to the CAN bus, and the instrument cluster or ADAS acts on it — typically inside 100 milliseconds, end to end.
